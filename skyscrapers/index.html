Privacy Policy

Skyscrapers: Tower Puzzle · Effective date: August 18, 2026

Skyscrapers: Tower Puzzle does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. There is nothing for us to disclose to you, sell, or lose in a breach, because we simply don't have it.

What the app does

Skyscrapers: Tower Puzzle is a single-player logic puzzle game. All puzzles are bundled inside the app itself; the app does not connect to any server, account system, or third-party service to function.

Data we collect

None. Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data to third-party services, SDKs, or ad networks

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs) of any kind.

Local storage

Your language preference and theme (light/dark) are stored only on your own device using standard local app storage, and are never transmitted anywhere. Puzzle progress exists only in memory while you play and is not saved or synced.

Permissions

The app may request standard permissions required by the Android platform for a WebView-based app to run. These permissions are not used to collect, transmit, or share any data.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind from anyone, this applies equally to children. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes - for example, if a future update adds a feature that involves data collection - this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to billogstudio@gmail.com.
